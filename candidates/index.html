What I Can Actually Offer You

Honest Conversations

I'll tell you what I think. If a role isn't right for you, I'll say so — even if it means I don't make a placement. Your career is too important for a forced fit.

Market Knowledge

I know what Supply Chain Directors earn in pharma versus FMCG. I know which sectors are hiring and which are contracting. I know what skills are commanding premiums right now. You'll get a real picture, not a sales pitch.

Confidentiality

Obvious. Essential. Non-negotiable. If you're in a senior role and exploring options, the last thing you need is your current employer finding out. Every conversation stays between us.

Role Level

Director, head-of, and C-suite level. These aren't junior positions.

I place people at director, head-of, and C-suite level. These are senior appointments in supply chain, operations, procurement, and transformation leadership.

Typical titles: Supply Chain Director, Head of Logistics, Chief Procurement Officer, Operations Director, Chief Transformation Officer, Interim Supply Chain Director (IR35-compliant).

Sectors: Consumer and Retail, Manufacturing, Pharmaceuticals, Construction. Geography: UK-wide, with particular depth in the Midlands, North West, and London.

If you're at this level and in these spaces, we should probably know each other. Even if the timing isn't right for a move now, it might be in six months.

How This Works

You get in touch. We have a conversation. Not a screening call where I tick boxes — an actual conversation about your career, what you're looking for, and what matters to you beyond the job title and the salary.

If I have something that fits, I'll tell you about it properly. The company. The team. The real reason the role exists. The things that a job ad doesn't say.

If I don't have something right now, that's fine too. I'll keep you in mind. And when the right role comes up — the one that genuinely matches what you're after — you'll hear from me. Not before.

I won't send your CV anywhere without your explicit permission. I won't put you forward for roles you haven't agreed to. And I won't disappear after the first conversation. That last one sounds basic, but apparently it needs saying.

What I Won't Do

Spam you with irrelevant roles. Ghost you after an interview. Send your details to companies you haven't approved. Treat you like a commodity.

I've been on the other side of bad recruitment practices for 20 years. I know what candidates hate because I've watched it happen. You won't get that from me.
