Consumer & Retail

FMCG, direct-to-consumer brands, high-growth retail. This is where pace kills.

I've worked with some of the UK's fastest-growing consumer brands. The hiring challenge in this sector isn't finding supply chain people. It's finding supply chain people who can operate at the speed these businesses demand.

A brand that's grown 300% in three years doesn't need a supply chain manager who's spent a decade at a company where nothing changed. They need someone who can build processes while the ground is shifting, manage omnichannel distribution complexity, and scale a logistics operation that was designed for a fraction of current volumes.

The talent pipeline in FMCG can't keep up. Growth has outstripped the supply of senior people who've actually done this at scale.

Manufacturing

58% of UK manufacturers are bringing operations back to British soil. That's not a forecast. It's happening now.

Reshoring is creating demand for supply chain and operations leaders that didn't exist three years ago. PwC projects 200,000 new UK jobs over the next decade from nearshoring and reshoring alone.

But here's the problem nobody talks about. You can build a new production line in 18 months. Finding the operations director to run it? That's the bottleneck. The UK manufacturing workforce is ageing. And the mid-level supply chain managers who should be stepping into senior roles? The apprenticeship levy was supposed to develop them. Only 4% of levy-paying logistics employers fully used their available funds.

I've been recruiting for manufacturing businesses across the Midlands, North West, and wider UK for my entire career.

Typical roles I fill:

Operations Director Head of Procurement Supply Chain Transformation Lead Automation Director

Pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceutical logistics in the UK is an 11.3 billion pound market. The government's Life Sciences plan targets 41 billion pounds in sector growth by 2035.

This is a sector where getting recruitment wrong doesn't just cost money. It costs compliance.

GDP-qualified drivers remain the principal bottleneck in pharmaceutical logistics capacity. Quality assurance roles sit unfilled for months. API synthesis specialists are so rare they might as well be mythical creatures. And the regulatory environment means you can't just drop someone from a consumer goods background into a pharma supply chain and hope for the best.

The major pharma clusters in the UK — Cambridge, Oxford, the North West — each have their own talent ecosystems. I recruit across all three.

Typical roles I fill:

Head of Supply Chain (GDP) Quality Assurance Director Procurement Director (Regulated) Cold Chain Manager

Construction

The CITB says the UK construction sector needs 239,300 additional workers over the next five years. Over 140,000 current vacancies are stalling housing and infrastructure projects right now.

Construction procurement is under extraordinary pressure. Major public and private projects need commercial specialists and procurement directors who can manage material cost inflation, post-Brexit compliance, and sustainable sourcing mandates simultaneously. That combination of skills barely existed five years ago. Today it's non-negotiable.

Before founding Lammond, I worked with clients across the built environment sector. I've placed senior leaders in environments where a delayed procurement decision doesn't just cost money. It delays housing. It stalls infrastructure.

Typical roles I fill:

Commercial Director Supply Chain Director Operations Director Sustainability Manager
