How I Measure Success

A placement isn't a success the day someone signs a contract. It's a success when they're still in the role twelve months later, performing, and the team around them is stronger for it. That's what I'm tracking. Not fill rates, not time-to-offer metrics that encourage cutting corners, not how many CVs I can send before you stop reading them.

The recruitment industry measures itself on volume. I measure myself on whether the hire actually worked. That difference shapes everything about how I approach a search — from the brief, to the shortlist, to the follow-up three months after they've started.
