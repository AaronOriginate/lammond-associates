How I Work

A visible process, not a black box

The Brief — Getting It Right

Before I look at a single candidate, I need to understand what the role actually needs, what your team is like, and what's happened before. If I don't understand the role well enough to explain it to a candidate in their language, I'm not ready to start the search.

Market Mapping — The Longlist

I map the market. Not a keyword search on LinkedIn. Proper identification of who's out there, where they are, and whether they'd move. A generalist searches for 'supply chain director' and gets 10,000 results. I know the 30 people who could do your specific role.

The Shortlist — Quality Over Volume

You'll receive a shortlist. Typically three to five candidates. Each one with a written assessment — not a recycled CV summary, but my honest view of their strengths, gaps, and why they're right for your specific role.

Offer & Onboarding — The Part Everyone Forgets

The offer stage is where deals fall apart. Counter-offers. Cold feet. I've seen it all across 20 years. And I don't stop at the signature — the first 90 days determine whether a placement sticks.
