The Short Version

My name's Tom Birrane. I run Lammond Associates from Derby.

Sport and Exercise Sciences degree from the University of Sunderland. Graduated in 2001. Walked into recruitment and never walked out.

That's the part nobody expects. A lad with a sports science background building a career in supply chain recruitment. But here's the thing — recruitment is about reading people, understanding pressure, knowing when someone's right for a situation and when they're not. I'd been doing that on training pitches before I ever did it in boardrooms.

The early years were spent learning the trade. SF Group. Distinct Recruitment in Derby. Proper apprenticeship stuff — cold calls, client visits, the kind of hands-on work where you either learn to listen or you starve.

Then Birmingham. Uniting Ambition appointed me Head of Supply Chain in 2014. That's where I started working exclusively with senior leadership — CSCOs, CPOs, Transformation Directors. The people who don't respond to generic InMails because they're too busy actually running supply chains.

And then Bis Henderson. If you're in supply chain and logistics, you know the name. Thirty-plus years in the sector, Generation Logistics sponsors, one of the most recognised specialist recruiters in the UK. Working there taught me what good looks like at scale. It also taught me what I wanted to do differently.

Why I Started Lammond

I started Lammond Associates in November 2020 because recruitment shouldn't be this broken.

That sounds dramatic. It isn't. Ask any ops director who's worked with agencies and they'll tell you the same story. Poor, prolonged processes. Transactional relationships. A complete lack of real communication. You brief someone, you chase them for a fortnight, you get a pile of CVs from people who've clearly never worked in your sector, and then the recruiter goes quiet.

I'd watched it happen for years. Good clients getting let down by lazy processes. Good candidates getting treated like inventory. And I thought: I know how to do this properly. I know supply chain. I know operations. I know procurement. I know the people, the companies, the sector-specific problems that a generalist recruiter can't even spell.

So I built something simple. One person. Direct accountability. No layers, no excuses. If you work with Lammond, you work with me. Not a junior doing the legwork while I'm at lunch.

The Honest Bit

I'm not the right recruiter for everyone. That's not false modesty. It's practical.

If you need 50 warehouse operatives by Friday, call an agency with a temp desk. That's not what I do. If you want the cheapest option and don't care who sends the CVs, I'm not your person either.

What I do is find senior supply chain and operations leaders — the kind of people who don't apply to job ads because they're too busy running supply chains. I find them through 20 years of relationships, not through keyword searches on a database. It takes longer. It costs more. And the results are the reason my clients come back.

I can't promise I'll fill every role. Nobody honest can. But I can promise you'll always know where things stand. No chasing. No silence. No surprises.
