What Happens Next

You get in touch. We have a conversation — usually 15 to 20 minutes. I'll ask about the role, the team, the timeline, and what's gone wrong before (if anything). You'll get a straight answer on whether I can help and what that looks like.

No pitch decks. No "let me send you our capabilities document." Just a direct conversation about what you actually need.

If it turns out I'm not the right fit, I'll say so. I'd rather be honest upfront than waste both our time pretending.
